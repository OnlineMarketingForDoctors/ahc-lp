      Your surgeon

      Dr Anastasios Vekris, MD, Plastic Surgeon

      Dr Vekris has worked with the FUE technique for more than 20 years. He founded Advanced Hair Clinics in Athens, and with his medical teams he has performed over 30,000 hair transplants, more than any other Greek doctor.

      He trained in hair restoration surgery under Dr J.C. Kim at KNU University Hospital in South Korea and furthered his training in London and San Francisco. He has operated in Greece, the UK, France, the USA and across the Middle East, and his patients include well-known names from entertainment, media and business.

      He is an invited speaker and trainer at the world’s leading hair restoration meetings, including ISHRS, IMCAS, the World FUE Institute and FUE Europe, and has taken part in more than 120 Greek and international conferences. Doctors and nurses from many countries train with his team in Athens every year.

        The Vekris Signature

        Questions patients ask before booking with Dr Vekris

        What does the Vekris Signature cost?

        It starts from €12,000. Your final price depends on the size and complexity of your procedure, including whether it takes one or two days, and is confirmed after your assessment. It is charged per session, with no cap on the number of grafts.

        Can my treatment stay discreet?

        Yes. Dr Vekris and his teams are among the few who successfully perform Unshaven FUE, where only the donor area is trimmed and grafts are placed between your existing hairs, so most patients return to work and social life within 24 to 48 hours. Long Hair FUE, where your hair is not cut at all, is also available.

        How long do I need to stay in Athens?

        Plan for two to three days. You arrive and have your consultation on day one, your procedure on day two, and your post-operative review and first wash on day three, after which you can normally fly home. Your exact schedule depends on your treatment plan.

        Can I have a consultation before I travel?

        Yes. Every international patient starts with an online consultation. You send photos of your hair, speak to a doctor by video and receive a personal plan and starting price before booking anything. Your final price is confirmed after your assessment in Athens.

        Will Dr Vekris really perform my hair transplant?

        Yes. With the Vekris Signature, Dr Vekris meets you for your consultation and final assessment, designs your hairline and performs your procedure himself, leading every step of your journey.

        Is it painful, and when can I fly home?

        The procedure is done under local anaesthesia and most patients only feel a slight pulling in the donor area for a day or two. You can normally fly home after your post-operative review on day three, following your doctor’s advice.

        When will I see my result?

        Some transplanted hairs shed in the first weeks, which is normal. New growth starts around the third month, around 70 to 80% of the result shows by four to six months, and the full result by twelve months.

        What happens if I have a question after I get home?

        Our team supports you before, during and after your visit, in English, French and German, by phone, email and WhatsApp. If you are near one of our clinics in Greece or Cyprus, you can also visit in person.

        Where will I stay?

        We recommend partner hotels close to the clinic, many of them within walking distance, and can suggest one that suits your plans.

        Can I travel alone?

        Yes. You do not need a companion for the procedure or your recovery, and our team is on hand throughout your stay.
