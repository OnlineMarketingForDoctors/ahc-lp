        Your trip, step by step

        From your first message to your final result

        1. At home

          Online consultation

          Send a few photos of your hair and talk to a doctor by video. You learn what is possible, how many sessions you need and which package suits you.

        2. At home

          Your plan and starting price

          You receive a personal treatment plan and a starting price. Once you choose your dates, we arrange your airport transfers and recommend hotels close to the clinic.

        3. Day 1

          Arrive in Athens

          We meet you at arrivals and take you to your hotel or straight to the clinic, depending on your flight.

        4. Day 1

          Consultation and final assessment

          Your doctor examines your scalp, confirms your plan and price and draws your new hairline around your face, age and the long-term pattern of your hair loss.

        5. Day 2

          Procedure day

          Your FUE hair transplant is performed exclusively by doctors under local anaesthesia. You can talk, listen to music or watch a film while you rest.

        6. Day 3

          Post-operative review and first wash

          The team checks your donor and recipient areas, washes your hair for the first time and explains how to look after it at home.

        7. Day 3

          Fly home

          After your review you can normally fly home, following your doctor’s advice. Most patients are back at work within a few days.

        8. Back home

          Support wherever you live

          Our team stays in touch and answers your questions in English, French or German as your new hair grows.

        9. Final result

          A natural result for life

          A hair transplant that does not look like a hair transplant. Most of the result shows by six months, and the full result by twelve.

        Travelling for your hair transplant

        Questions international patients ask us

        What does the €3,000 package include?

        The Advanced Team package is a complete hair transplant package for up to [X,XXX] grafts. It includes your consultation and final assessment in Athens, your FUE hair transplant performed by doctors trained by Dr Vekris, airport pick-up and departure transfers, your post-operative review and first wash at no extra cost, hotel recommendations close to the clinic and support in English, French and German.

        How long do I need to stay in Athens?

        Plan for two to three days. You arrive and have your consultation on day one, your procedure on day two, and your post-operative review and first wash on day three, after which you can normally fly home. Your exact schedule depends on your treatment plan.

        Can I have a consultation before I travel?

        Yes. Every international patient starts with an online consultation. You send photos of your hair, speak to a doctor by video and receive a personal plan and starting price before booking anything. Your final price is confirmed after your assessment in Athens.

        Who will actually perform my hair transplant?

        Only doctors perform hair transplants at Advanced Hair Clinics. In the Advanced Team package, your procedure is performed by our doctors, trained by Dr Vekris. If you choose the Vekris Signature upgrade, Dr Vekris performs it himself.

        How does the cost compare with Turkey?

        Some clinics in Turkey advertise lower headline prices. Our complete package starts from €3,000, with every step performed by doctors under EU medical standards and what is included set out before you travel. Whichever clinic you consider, ask who will perform your procedure.

        Is it painful, and when can I fly home?

        The procedure is done under local anaesthesia and most patients only feel a slight pulling in the donor area for a day or two. You can normally fly home after your post-operative review on day three, following your doctor’s advice.

        When will I see my result?

        Some transplanted hairs shed in the first weeks, which is normal. New growth starts around the third month, around 70 to 80% of the result shows by four to six months, and the full result by twelve months.

        What happens if I have a question after I get home?

        Our team supports you before, during and after your visit, in English, French and German, by phone, email and WhatsApp. If you are near one of our clinics in Greece or Cyprus, you can also visit in person.

        Where will I stay?

        We recommend partner hotels close to the clinic, many of them within walking distance, and can suggest one that suits your plans.

        Can I travel alone?

        Yes. You do not need a companion for the procedure or your recovery, and our team is on hand throughout your stay.
