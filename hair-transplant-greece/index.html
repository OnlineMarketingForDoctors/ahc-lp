      Dr Vekris has worked with the FUE technique for more than 20 years. He founded Advanced Hair Clinics in Athens, and with his medical teams he has performed over 30,000 hair transplants, more than any other Greek doctor.

        Your trip, step by step

        From your first message to your final result

        1. At home

          Online consultation

          Send a few photos of your hair and talk to a doctor by video. You learn what is possible, how many sessions you need and which package suits you.

        4. Day 2

          Your doctor examines your scalp, confirms the plan and draws your new hairline around your face, age and the long-term pattern of your hair loss.

          Your FUE hair transplant is performed exclusively by doctors under local anaesthesia. You can talk, listen to music or watch a film while you rest.

        9. Final result

          A natural result for life

          A hair transplant that does not look like a hair transplant. Most of the result shows by six months, and the full result by twelve.

        Travelling for your hair transplant

        Questions international patients ask us

        How long do I need to stay in Athens?

        Most patients stay four nights: one to arrive, one for the consultation and procedure, one for the check-up and first wash, and a spare day before flying. Larger sessions over two days add a night.

        Can I have a consultation before I travel?

        Yes. Every international patient starts with an online consultation. You send photos of your hair, speak to a doctor by video and receive a written plan and price before booking anything.

        Who will actually perform my hair transplant?

        Only doctors perform hair transplants at Advanced Hair Clinics. In the Advanced Team package, your procedure is performed by doctors trained by Dr Vekris. In the Vekris Signature package, Dr Vekris performs it himself.

        Why do you price per session and not per graft?

        A patient cannot count the grafts implanted, so pricing per graft is hard to check. We price by the session needed to cover your area with the best safe density, with no upper limit on grafts.

        Is it painful, and when can I fly home?

        The procedure is done under local anaesthesia and most patients only feel a slight pulling in the donor area for a day or two. You can usually fly home the day after your check-up.

        When will I see my result?

        Some transplanted hairs shed in the first weeks, which is normal. New growth starts around the third month, around 70 to 80% of the result shows by four to six months, and the full result by twelve months.

        What happens if I have a question after I get home?

        Your coordinator and our doctors stay in touch for 12 months by video, phone, email and WhatsApp. If you are near one of our clinics in Greece or Cyprus, you can also visit in person.
